Meet the team

A diverse group of researchers, engineers, and educators pushing the boundaries of distributed computing.

Mahyar T. Moghaddam

Associate Professor

Head of DECO Lab and Associate Professor at SDU, working on AI-enabled systems engineering, distributed green computing, and human-centered software engineering. He leads sustainability projects such as Nudge2Green and served as Organizing Chair of ICSA 2025.

mtmo@mmmi.sdu.dk

Mikkel Baun Kjærgaard

LEGO® Chair and Professor

LEGO® Chair and Professor in Software Engineering at SDU's Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute, and Vice-Head of Research across SDU Software Engineering, the Centre for Industrial Software, and the Centre for Software Technology. His research applies data-science and machine-learning methods to ubiquitous and cyber-physical systems, with contributions spanning IoT, robotics, and smart buildings.

mbkj@mmmi.sdu.dk

Jakob Hviid

Assistant Professor

Works at the intersection of software architecture and AI, using ontologies and knowledge graphs as semantic foundations for context-aware systems. Current directions include knowledge-driven architectures for self-managing AI and ontology-grounded reasoning for AI in critical energy infrastructure.

jah@mmmi.sdu.dk

Francesco Daghero

Postdoctoral Researcher

Researches efficient machine learning across both resource-constrained systems, such as TinyML, and high-performance computing platforms. His work targets energy and memory efficiency, from algorithm design to compiler and system-level optimization.

fdag@mmmi.sdu.dk

Tiziano Santilli

Postdoctoral Researcher

Investigates software architecture and generative AI, integrating large and small language models to automate architectural recovery and system documentation. He designs compliance-aware architectures that treat ethical and safety constraints, including those of the EU AI Act, as first-class guardrails in regulated domains.

tisa@mmmi.sdu.dk

Emil Jørgensen Njor

Assistant Professor

Researching AI on extremely resource-constrained devices, with a focus on scaling adoption and addressing the nuanced challenges and benefits of the technology.

njor@mmmi.sdu.dk

Anders L. B. Petersen

PhD Student

Works at the intersection of machine learning, software architecture, and reliable AI systems. He combines a background in mathematical modelling from DTU with over four years of industry experience designing intelligent systems.

anbae@mmmi.sdu.dk
